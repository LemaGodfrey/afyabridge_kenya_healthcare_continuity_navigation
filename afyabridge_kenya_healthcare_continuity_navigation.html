Systems Research & Architectural Justification

Solving Kenya's Hospital-to-Home Continuity & SHA Navigation Gap

1. Core Problem Identified Across Kenyan Households & Level 4/5 Hospitals

Patients across Nairobi, Kajiado, and county referral networks face a critical "Revolving-Door & Blind-Referral Breakdown":

  • At the Hospital Gate: Families spend scarce cash on transport only to encounter SHA contracting confusion, broken X-ray/lab equipment, or essential drug stock-outs—forcing out-of-pocket chemist purchases at inflated retail prices.
  • At Home Post-Discharge: Paper discharge summaries are easily lost or misunderstood. Patients with chronic NCDs (hypertension, diabetes) or post-maternity needs miss danger signs at home, while Community Health Promoters (CHPs) lack real-time hospital discharge rosters—leading to preventable readmissions.

2. How AfyaBridge Kenya Closes the Loop

  • Pre-Travel Facility & SHA Transparency: Real-time Level 4/5 resource status (SHA active flag, ER queue, insulin/X-ray/theatre readiness) plus an ECCIF statutory rights pass.
  • Unified Discharge & Fair Chemist Pricing: Clinicians push structured regimens directly to the patient's phone and assigned Ward CHP, complete with KES retail price benchmarks.
  • Offline-Ready CHP Escalation Loop: CHPs log household vitals (BP, blood sugar) even offline and trigger instant red-flag alerts back to Level 4 triage desks when thresholds breach.
